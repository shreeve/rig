# std.json: `\u` runs two at a time, and indentation as yyjson takes it (2026-11-07)

The rows where std.json still trailed yyjson on the Mac after #984: twitter and twitterescaped (about 19%) and github_events (about 10%).

## Where the time went (main 5891ebfac, Time Profiler, by outer function)

| file | document | skip_plain_ascii | escaped | utf8_text | List.push (out of line) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| twitter | 65% | 16% | 2.9% | 7.5% | 6.3% |
| twitterescaped | 41% | 14% | 39% | | 3.9% |
| github_events | 65% | 25% | 5.3% | | 4.2% |

- **twitterescaped** spends 39% in `escaped`: 31,770 of its 31,818 `\u` escapes are characters of three UTF-8 bytes, in runs of 18.5 on average, and each went through `escape`, `unicode` (a call), `hex4`, and two pushes.
- **twitter** spends about 16% on whitespace lines: its indentation is 6, 8 or 10 spaces on most lines, and each change of depth mispredicts the exit of the 8-space or the 2-space loop. yyjson takes indentation as 16 pair tests in a row, each a branch of its own.

## Ceilings (hand-edited Zig), against main, fastest of 20 interleaved runs

| arm | what | result |
|---|---|---|
| u1 | runs of three-byte `\u` escapes two at a time: one read, one 6-byte write | twitterescaped 10.9% faster (8.74 to 7.62 instructions a byte, below yyjson's 7.94); others level |
| u2 | u1 writing through a local cursor | no better than u1 |
| w1 | `skip_indent` after a value too, not only after `,` and `[` | citm_catalog 3.2%, github_events 2.6% faster; others level |
| p1 | the tape's push inlined at std.json's own sites only | github_events 2.8% faster, twitter 0.6%; needs new syntax, not built |
| i1 (on u1 + w1) | 16 pair tests in a row after a line break, as yyjson | citm_catalog 2.6%, github_events 3.7%, gsoc-2018 1.6% faster; others level (two runs) |
| i3 | the pairs as one loop, no 8-space step | 3-26% slower |
| i7 | 8 pair tests in a row, then the loops | 3-6% slower on the indented files |

The pair tests pay only as separate branches, 16 of them: as a loop, or 8 of them, they lose.

## What lands (Rig source, std/json.rig)

- `three_byte_run`: from a backslash, a run of `\uXXXX` escapes whose characters each take three UTF-8 bytes, two at a time: one U128 read, one 6-byte write. Anything else goes to `escape` as before. `unicode`'s own pair path, which this replaces, is gone.
- `skip_indent` after a value, in place of `skip_space`.
- `skip_pairs`: after a line break with 32 bytes left, up to 16 pair tests in a row, from two U128 reads (16 reads of a U16 at `at + 2k` kept their bounds checks: 31% more instructions on citm_catalog).
