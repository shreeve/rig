# std.json: a key's colon at once, an inlined key, and an integer's early exit (2026-11-06)

The json panel's items 4 (the key path) and 6 (an integer fast exit), panel/SYNTHESIS.md. Parsers kept in `key-int/`: `keyB.rig`, `keyC.rig`, `keyD.rig` (the three key paths below), `intD.rig` (what lands).

## What lands
- **`key` is inlined after a comma**, where most keys are, as `key_at`; an object's first key, after its `{`, calls it out of line through `key`. Inlined at both sites, LLVM left `key` out of line in `document` with any change to it.
- **A key ending at `:` with the value's first byte next returns at once** (`end + 1`); `: ` and other whitespace go through `skip_space` as before.
- **An integer with no fraction, no exponent, and at most 18 digits** (which always fit) goes on the tape as soon as its digits end. The test sits in the `else` of the test for `.`, so a float pays nothing for it: placed before that test, canada, all floats, took 5% more instructions.
