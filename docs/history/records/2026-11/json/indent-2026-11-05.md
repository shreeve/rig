# std.json: indentation taken after a separator (2026-11-05)

The panel's first lead ([panel/SYNTHESIS.md](panel/SYNTHESIS.md), item 1), built and timed on the Mac at Rig main cf6a362a6.

## The change

After `,` or an opening bracket, `document` calls `skip_indent` where it called `skip_space`. A pretty-printer puts a line break and the next line's indentation there, so `skip_indent` takes:
- a byte that begins a token at once: minified input has no whitespace there;
- else a `\n`, then 8 spaces at a time (`SPACES`), then 2 at a time (`PAIR`);
- the rest, if any, by `skip_space` as before.

Every step is a compare against a fixed pattern, and each advance is a constant, so the processor predicts the branches and runs ahead of them. The skips that `skip-space-2026-11-05.md` rejected computed each advance from the bytes instead.

## What was tried

| variant | what | result |
|---|---|---|
| the panel's | `skip_indent` with no first-byte test, and keys taking `: ` directly | 9-18% faster on the indented files, but 10% slower on twitterescaped and 2% slower on canada (both minified) |
| **A, taken** | `skip_indent` with the first-byte test; keys as before | faster on the indented files, level on the minified ones |
| B | A, and keys taking a `:` directly | as fast as A or slower on five of six files |
