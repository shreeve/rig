# A3: data layout, caching, memory hierarchy

**Finding.** Trig is not stall-bound: its IPC beats tsrs's (2.51 against 2.34), and its extra LLC misses cost at most about 3%. Layout costs Trig **instructions** (dependent loads, tests, wide keys) and **page faults** (83K against 17.7K).

## Records

- **Type.** tsrs keeps a 24 B header and its payload in one allocation (`types.rs:1338`). In Trig, target, mapper and arguments are 2 dependent loads into other tables (`store_generated.rig:1067-1092`).
- **Symbol.** tsrs: 32 B, one load. Trig, binder symbol: `sym_loc` → `bindings[f]` → `symbols[l]` (64 B, 3 bounds tests). Trig, checker symbol: `syms` → `symbol_data` (`checker.rig:405-460`).
- **Mapper.** tsrs: 16 B. Trig: a 24 B stride in the annotation. Its untyped U32 fields re-test `Slot.of` on every read.
- **Relation key.** tsrs: an 8 B slot (`relater_types.rs:116`). Trig: a 24 B entry, an 8 B hash and a 5 B index, with 3 arrays per probe.
- **Huge pages.** tsrs advises its arena `MADV_HUGEPAGE` (`reserve.rs:118`) and gets them on pup (fallback 51 of 28,815). Rig's Tables advise **`MADV_NOHUGEPAGE`** (`table.zig:102`).

## Ranked (ts-pattern, 13.22G instructions)

| # | technique | evidence | win | owner | cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Decorated sort: build each symbol's key (declaration, file, position, name, Sym) once | tsrs `utilities.rs:479`. Trig: compare_symbols 350M, sort+merge 179M, text ~100M | −350…−450M | Trig | small; exact |
| 2 | Huge pages: on each Table past its first 2 MiB, and on 2 MiB-aligned large blocks | tsrs: faults 30× fewer, wall −4.4%. Trig: dTLB misses 3.2× tsrs's | −4…6% time | Rig | small; copy tsrs's shape (compaction stalls before) |
| 3 | Active-mapper caches as a stack that does not move (no 104 B Map moves) | `ListOf(Map).push` 189M; tsrs `checker_11.rs:1453` | −150…−190M | Trig | tiny |
| 4 | One-hop symbol and node columns: program-wide Tables built in `Ids.of` | members+symbols +371M; is_declaration_contained_by 303M | −150…−300M | Trig | medium |
| 5 | Target and mapper into the Type row; rare fields to side tables; StructuredType only on resolve | tsrs `types.rs:2122-2340` | −100…−200M, less RSS | Trig (gen-store.py) | medium |
| 6 | Relation cache as `Map[U64,U8]` keyed `s\|t<<27\|state<<54`; with TYPES=2^27 every key fits | tsrs −0.8% instructions | −100…−150M | Trig | small |
| 7 | Value-only hash index (hashbrown `HashTable<T>`, 4–8 B slots) | tsrs ReferenceInstantiations; `get_type_items_instantiation_key` 137M | −150…−300M | Rig, then Trig | medium; overlaps Map survey |
| 8 | Mapper in 16 B with typed Slot fields | mapper_map +95M | −30…−80M | Trig | small |
| 9 | Type lists as a Table plus `Span` | `type_at` is a tested List index | −50…−150M | Rig, then Trig | medium; overlaps BCE |
| 10 | A niche for `Slot[n]` | `runtime.zig:440` | RSS only | Rig | small |

**Rejected:** Flag bits in ids (order is by id); struct-of-arrays for flags (read together, and misses are not the bottleneck); interning (tsrs measured a loss).

**Total:** items 1 and 3–8 save about 1.0–1.7G instructions (8–13%), and item 2 saves 4–6% of time.
