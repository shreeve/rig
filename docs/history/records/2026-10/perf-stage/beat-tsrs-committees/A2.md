# A2: allocation and copies

Evidence: the tg.script stacks, `perf annotate` of t.data, tsrs source, Rig's alloc.zig and runtime.zig. tsrs on ts-pattern: 3.63M instantiations.

## Findings

1. **189M of the "push" is Map moves, not growth.** `ListOf(Map(CacheKey,u32)).push` runs in `push_active_mapper` and `pop_active_mapper` (members.rig:2995). 233 of its 248 samples come from `instantiate_type_with_alias`. Each new-mapper instantiation moves the 104-byte Map header four times, through overlapping ymm stack stores. tsrs moves a 32-byte `PackedMap` (checker_11.rs:1441); Go copies nothing.
2. **Allocator fast path: alloc about 48 instructions, free about 55, over about 11M calls.** mimalloc takes about 15–20 (Leijen et al. 2019). Rig's extra work:
   - a full frame, because the slow paths are inlined;
   - a noinline `heap()`;
   - `mustOwn`, a check that is true by construction;
   - a bounds check on `free[c]`.
3. **Wasted remaps.** `reallocSlice` tries `rawRemap` first, but a doubling small block always changes class: `resize` (37M) and `remap` (15M) are wasted.
4. **Growth chains.** Rig's growth policy matches Rust's `Vec` (minimum 4, doubling). Rust's `collect` reserves exactly. Trig fills by push loops (`table_entries`, the `items` temporaries). Grow and realloc cost about 146M.
5. **memcpyFast is mostly the Relater:** 76 of its 173 samples. `get_relater` and `put_relater` (relater.rig:1676) copy about 290 bytes three or four times. tsrs uses a 4-byte `P<Relater>` on an intrusive free list (relater_2.rs:27).
6. **Teardown at exit: 0.5%.** These are drop frees under `emit_files_and_report_errors`. tsrs never frees (ptr.rs: "Destructors never run").
7. **Arenas.** Trig's id stores already work like tsrs's leak arena. What is left are short-lived temporaries.

## Recommendations

| # | change | owner | win (instructions) | cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A leaf `alloc`/`free`: read `my_heap` inline (safe, since `alloc` never switches) and keep a cold, noinline slow path. Drop `mustOwn` there (proved). Index `free` by `u8` over 256 slots, so the type proves the bound. | Rig | 250–350M (2–2.6%) | ~60 lines |
| 2 | Keep the mapper caches in place: a depth count, reuse, and clear only when `len > 0`. | Trig | 150–250M | ~20 lines |
| 3 | Skip remap for small blocks that grow; fuse alloc, copy and free into one `grow` call. | Rig | 50–70M | ~15 lines |
| 4 | The Relater in a box (`List[^Relater]`), then general move elision. | Trig, then Rig | 40–60M | small, then medium |
| 5 | Skip memory-only drops at exit, through a classifier. | Rig + Trig | ~65M | medium |
| 6 | Compiler presize for one push per iteration (capacity is not semantics), and `table_entries` by hand. | Rig / Trig | 50–100M | medium / trivial |
| 7 | A bump region: defer; item 1 gets most of its gain. | — | — | — |

Together these save about 600–850M, bringing Trig's 1048M near tsrs's 344M.
